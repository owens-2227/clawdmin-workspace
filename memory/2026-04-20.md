## 07:12 — Investigated & Fixed Next.js MongoDB Connection Pool

**Three problems found and fixed:**

1. **Stale connection pool (root cause of hanging):** `db.ts` used bare `new MongoClient(uri)` with default settings — `socketTimeoutMS=0` (infinite!), `maxIdleTimeMS=0` (connections never expire). After Atlas maintenance/failover, stale connections became zombies. Fixed by adding explicit pool config: `maxPoolSize: 10`, `minPoolSize: 1`, `maxIdleTimeMS: 60s`, `socketTimeoutMS: 45s`, `retryWrites/retryReads: true`.

2. **Module-scoped singleton wiped by Next.js:** The `let client` / `let db` pattern can get reset by Next.js module re-evaluation. Moved to `globalThis` pattern (standard Next.js recommended approach).

3. **Sequential aggregations in `computeAllMetrics()`:** 7 MongoDB aggregations were running sequentially. Wrapped in `Promise.all()` — cut aggregation time from sequential to ~6.6s parallel.

**Additional optimizations:**
- Added `discoveredAt` index on `pain_point_posts` (date range queries)
- Added unique compound index `painPointId + snapshotDate` on `pain_point_snapshots` (bulkWrite upserts)
- Both indexes were missing

**Performance after fixes:**
- Cold request: 26s (was timing out at 30s)
- Warm request: 20-21s
- 3/3 sequential requests succeed (was 3/5 failing before)
- Remaining bottleneck: `bulkWrite` of 7480 snapshot upserts to Atlas free tier (~9s). Can't optimize further without upgrading Atlas tier.
